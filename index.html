About DoBu
Why Choose DoBu

TRAIN WITH PURPOSE.

Train with experienced instructors, explore our facilities and become part of the DoBu community.

Qualified Coaches

Six instructors cover martial arts and fitness, with qualifications including Dan grades, accredited coaching and sports science expertise.

Flexible Weekly Training

The scenario timetable provides training across seven days, with martial arts, kids sessions, private tuition and open mat practice.

Dedicated Facilities

Train in a large matted area with a fully-equipped gym, sauna, steam room and changing and shower facilities.

Training Options

Choose from Jiu-jitsu, Karate, Judo, Muay Thai, junior sessions, self-defence and fitness training.

Clear Membership Choices

Membership options start from £25 per month, with different session limits and an unlimited Elite option.

Community

Members can connect through the forum concept and social media integration, supporting a stronger training community.
